# Create your AWS account

## INTRODUCTION
This repository provides the foundational prerequisites and account setup guide required to get started with the AWS Cloud Computing course. 
Follow the instructions below to prepare your environment, configure your AWS account, and secure your access before beginning the modules.

## PREREQUISITE & REQUIREMENTS

Before deploying or interacting with AWS resources, ensure you have the following tools and account details ready:

**Personal Details:** A valid full name, email address, physical address, and phone number.

**Payment Method:** A valid Mastercard.

**Alternative Options:** If you do not have a Mastercard, you can create a virtual card via [GrabrFi](https://www.grabrfi.com) (requires a National Identification Number / NIN) or [Grey](https://grey.co) (requires a $5 initial deposit before use).

**Development Environment:** A local Integrated Development Environment (IDE) such as VS Code or PyCharm.

**Security Tooling:** The Google Authenticator app (available on the Google Play Store or Apple App Store) for configuring Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).


## SETUP & DEPLOYMENT INSTRUCTIONS

1. **Review Official Documentation:** 
   Familiarize yourself with the official AWS onboarding workflow via the [AWS Account Creation Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/accounts/latest/reference/getting-started.html).

2. **Register Your AWS Account:** 
   Navigate to the [AWS Sign-Up Portal](https://signin.aws.amazon.com/signup?request_type=register) and complete the registration form using your personal details and Mastercard.

3. **Configure Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** 
   Once your account is active, link it to the Google Authenticator app on your mobile device to secure your root and IAM user access.

4. **Prepare Your Local IDE:** 
   Open your preferred IDE (e.g., VS Code, PyCharm) to begin cloning course repositories and deploying cloud infrastructure.
